The microenvironment behind the prediction

Explore fixed TME feature maps alongside saved held-out predictions. Every point is a patient; coordinates stay fixed as the model, shot count or overlay changes.

Histologic subtype

Coordinates use all cohort covariates for an unsupervised, descriptive projection. Labels and predictions never enter UMAP/PCA. Every prediction comes from that patient’s own held-out fold. Class separation alone does not show prediction benefit.
Methods, coverage and interpretation

Fixed transformed TME measurements are averaged over available slides per patient, then imputed and standardized across cohort patients for display only. Classifier inputs and training-only preprocessing are unchanged. UMAP uses 15 neighbors, minimum distance 0.3, Euclidean distance, 500 epochs and seed 20260915, without searching parameters for class separation. PCA uses the same inputs. Different cohort maps are not aligned.

Corrected: Native wrong and TME correct; worsened: Native correct and TME wrong, using a fixed probability threshold of 0.5. Continuous changes are signed differences in true-class probability, in percentage points. These differ from AUROC. AUROC is the mean across the available matched folds, not a pooled score. Partial coverage is explicitly marked and missing patients are gray. Both negative and positive outcomes remain available. The map is descriptive, not a causal attribution map or a new held-out classifier.

Historical BRCA uses morph64; the other cohorts use core62. This offline viewer contains anonymous point numbers and saved probabilities, without patient IDs, raw measurements, private paths, external fonts or analytics. Publication figures and full provenance are exported separately.
