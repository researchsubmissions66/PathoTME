Methods and interpretation

Primary Spearman correlations use the exact cohort-median-imputed and standardized patient matrix used for the UMAP display. Pairwise-observed estimates omit patients missing either member of the feature pair; exact patient counts are shown on hover. Changing the estimate preserves the primary feature order. Average ranks handle ties. Complete linkage uses 1 − |ρ|, grouping strong negative and positive relationships; the heatmap always displays the signed value. A complete-link cut at |ρ| ≥ 0.85 defines aggregate clusters, with singleton features retained.

PCA uses standardized covariates, not the rank-correlation matrix. Effective rank is exp(−sum(p log p)); participation ratio is 1/sum(p²), where p is each covariance eigenvalue's normalized mass. These measure effective linear dimension and are not counts of independent biological signals. No labels, predictions, classifier fitting or outcome-based feature selection enter this analysis.

This viewer contains only feature names and aggregate statistics, without patient IDs, measurements, private paths, external fonts or analytics. Exact names, every pair and full PCA loadings are available in the accompanying CSV files.
