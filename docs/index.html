02 / THE BIOLOGICAL VOCABULARY

Measurements with
a reason to be here.

The panels are hand-defined by biological role and the available OpenTME measurements. They describe tissue organization, cellular composition, and local interactions.

How the features were chosen

  1. Start with observable structure.Use quantitative tissue and cell phenotypes rather than identifiers, class labels, or project metadata.
  2. Group related measurements.Organize measurements into coherent tokens instead of assigning a feature to a handpicked prompt phrase.
  3. Keep selection separate from fitting.Use fixed panels; fit imputation and normalization on each fold’s training slides only.

These panels express a biological hypothesis. The study does not establish that they are an optimal or exhaustive feature selection.
