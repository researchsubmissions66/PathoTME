# Quantitative TME figure exports

These static figures accompany the PathoTME research code. They contain aggregate or anonymous visualizations, not patient identifiers, raw TME tables, slide features, or model checkpoints. Each figure is available as vector PDF and high-resolution PNG.

## Feature structure

- [Four-cohort redundancy atlas](feature-redundancy/tme_redundancy_atlas.pdf): clustered feature correlations and biological families.
- [PCA variance curves](feature-redundancy/pca_variance_curves.pdf): variance explained by successive principal components.
- Clustered Spearman matrices: [NSCLC](feature-redundancy/nsclc_clustered_spearman.pdf), [BRCA](feature-redundancy/brca_clustered_spearman.pdf), [CRC](feature-redundancy/crc_clustered_spearman.pdf), [BLCA](feature-redundancy/blca_clustered_spearman.pdf).

The 62-feature core panel is used for NSCLC, CRC, and BLCA; BRCA uses its separate 64-feature morphology panel. The numbers of components explaining 90% of standardized feature variance are 19, 20, 17, and 17, respectively. Strong feature pairs with absolute Spearman correlation at least 0.85 number 29, 32, 27, and 48. These are descriptive summaries of the cohort feature matrices.

## Phenotype maps and saved predictions

- [Four-cohort UMAP atlas](tme-maps/tme_umap_atlas.pdf) and [PCA atlas](tme-maps/tme_pca_atlas.pdf): separately fitted, unsupervised cohort projections of patient-level TME measurements.
- [ViLa-MIL / CLIP-RN50 16-shot prediction changes](tme-maps/tme_prediction_changes_vila_rn50_16shot.pdf): held-out Native and measured-TME adapter predictions for NSCLC and BRCA. The adapter corrects 126 and worsens 67 NSCLC patients; it corrects 145 and worsens 71 BRCA patients, at the fixed 0.5 threshold.
- Full-panel TME-only LR shot maps: [NSCLC](tme-maps/nsclc_lr_allshot_umap.pdf), [BRCA](tme-maps/brca_lr_allshot_umap.pdf), [CRC](tme-maps/crc_lr_allshot_umap.pdf), [BLCA](tme-maps/blca_lr_allshot_umap.pdf).

UMAP coordinates are transductive displays fitted without labels or predictions; they are not held-out classifier features or evidence of biological clusters. The saved-prediction overlay uses only ViLa-MIL and does not include historical HiVE-MIL results. The private interactive atlas is excluded because its broader historical inventory contains older HiVE-MIL records. Current HiVE-MIL comparisons must use the October 3 refresh.

The exports were generated by the source workflows in `campaigns/feature_redundancy_20260915/` and `campaigns/tme_maps_20260915/`. The figures are descriptive and do not establish causal attribution or external-cohort generalization.
